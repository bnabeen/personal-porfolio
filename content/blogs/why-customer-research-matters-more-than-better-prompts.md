The best prompt cannot rescue a weak understanding of the customer.

Prompt engineering is often treated like magic, but in practice, prompt quality is strictly bounded by the clarity of your inputs:
- Who is experiencing this problem?
- What exact language do they use when describing their frustration?
- What alternative solutions have they tried and abandoned?

When you have real customer research, writing effective prompts becomes straightforward. Context beats clever phrasing every single time.
