AI didn't make content generic. It simply made generic thinking easier to produce at scale.

When everyone has access to the exact same models trained on the exact same common web data, the default output tends toward the statistical average of all human writing.

To stand out:
- **Bring proprietary observations:** Real data, real conversations, and specific lessons learned from execution.
- **Form strong opinions:** Don't let the model sand off the edges of your argument.
- **Use AI for iteration, not ideation:** Start with original human insight, then use tools to accelerate delivery.
